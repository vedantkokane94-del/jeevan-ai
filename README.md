## About The Project

JEEVAN AI is a government-scale emergency response platform designed to improve public safety during one of the world's largest religious gatherings—**Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2027**.

Instead of responding only after emergencies happen, JEEVAN AI combines Artificial Intelligence, Real-Time Mapping, Predictive Analytics, and Emergency Coordination to help authorities make faster and smarter decisions while helping pilgrims receive immediate assistance.

This project is being developed as part of **Kumbhathon Innovation S.P.R.I.N.T. 2026**.

---

# The Problem

During Kumbh Mela, millions of pilgrims travel across Nashik and Trimbakeshwar within a short period of time.

Major challenges include:

- Ambulances getting delayed because of crowd congestion.
- Slow emergency reporting.
- Difficulty locating unconscious people.
- Overloaded medical camps.
- Lost children and elderly citizens.
- Lack of real-time operational visibility.
- Heatstroke and dehydration incidents.
- Inefficient resource allocation.

Traditional emergency systems react after an incident.

JEEVAN AI focuses on predicting and responding faster.

Transform **Nashik Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2027** from reactive emergency management into predictive public health intelligence.

# AI Intelligence

## Crowd Risk Prediction

The AI continuously analyzes:

- Crowd density
- Temperature
- Incident frequency
- Medical camp load
- Ambulance availability

Example:

Ramkund

Risk Score: 91/100

Priority: High

---

## Smart Ambulance Routing

Instead of choosing the nearest ambulance, the AI considers:

- Traffic
- Crowd congestion
- Road closures
- Emergency corridors
- Hospital capability

The goal is to reduce response time.

---

## Medical Demand Forecast

Predicts emergency demand for:

- Next 30 minutes
- Next 60 minutes
- Next 2 hours

Each prediction includes a confidence score.
